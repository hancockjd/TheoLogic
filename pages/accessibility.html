Accessibility

Draft — no effective date

Accurate as built — but no conformance claim yet Unlike the other policy pages, the technical content below describes what is actually implemented in this site's code. It is safe to publish once the outstanding items are closed.

What must not be published yet is a conformance claim. Stating "this site conforms to WCAG 2.1 Level AA" is a representation, and the audit supporting it has not been done. Leave the commitment worded as an aim until an audit is complete.

Theo Logic, LLC intends this site to be usable by everyone, including people who navigate by keyboard, use a screen reader, or need reduced motion. Accessibility is treated as part of the build rather than a retrofit.

What is implemented

  • Keyboard navigation — every interactive control is reachable and operable by keyboard, with a visible focus indicator.
  • Skip link — a "Skip to content" link is the first focusable element on each page.
  • Landmarks and headings — content is structured with navigation, main, and footer landmarks, and each section is associated with its own heading.
  • Images — every meaningful image carries alternative text; decorative graphics are hidden from assistive technology.
  • The Vault step viewer follows the standard tab pattern, including arrow-key, Home, and End navigation.
  • The mobile menu leaves the keyboard tab order when closed and can be dismissed with Escape.
  • Forms — every field has an associated label, required fields are marked, and submission results are announced.
  • Reduced motion — animation is suppressed for visitors whose system requests it.
  • Without JavaScript the page still renders its full content.

Known limitations

Stated plainly, because an accessibility page that lists only successes is not useful to the people who need it:

  • Color contrast has not been formally measured. Small muted-grey text on the dark background — used for eyebrow labels and metadata — is the most likely failure and is being checked.
  • No assistive-technology testing has been performed with real screen readers. Automated checks are not a substitute.
  • The site has not been audited against WCAG by an independent reviewer.
  • Media alternatives are not yet in place. When trailers and scores are published, captions, transcripts, and audio description become necessary. Budget for this before video ships.

Standard we are working toward

The target is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. Do not upgrade this from an aim to a conformance claim until an audit supports it.

Tell us where it fails

If any part of this site is difficult or impossible for you to use, please say so — including which page, what you were trying to do, and the browser or assistive technology you use. Reports are treated as defects.

Provide a monitored contact address and a target response time. Confirm whether hello@theologic.com is correct, or whether accessibility reports should route elsewhere.

Formal complaints

Describe the escalation route if someone is not satisfied with the response. Some jurisdictions require this, and the requirement may depend on whether the company contracts with public institutions — plausible given the KINDNESS Project's classroom materials.
