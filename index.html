The Vault

Send us your idea.
Keep your rights.

Most companies ask you to email a pitch into the void. The Vault is a controlled intake channel: encrypted submission, a countersigned NDA before anyone reads a word, a timestamped record of exactly what you sent and when, and a documented decision at the end.

Encrypted submission

Scripts, treatments, tracks, and decks upload over an encrypted channel into isolated storage. Nothing lands in a shared inbox, and nothing is readable by staff until the agreement in step two is executed on both sides.

  • TLS in transit, encrypted at rest
  • Isolated per-submission storage
  • File type and size validation on upload

E-SIGNATURE   NDAs, submission releases, option agreements, and deal memos route through the company's existing document-signing platform, so every executed agreement lands in the same system of record your legal and finance teams already use.
